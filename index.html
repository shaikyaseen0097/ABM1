Integrated Problem Addressed

Fixed-threshold voltage monitors can fail to detect early over-voltage or under-voltage conditions when the battery operates under different modes. Conventional directed tests can also miss adaptive threshold transitions.

Proposed RTL Innovation

An adaptive voltage threshold controller dynamically selects monitoring limits according to the current operating mode, improving scenario-aware detection and making threshold transitions explicit for verification.
